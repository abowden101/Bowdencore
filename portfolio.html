What an engagement looks like

A typical deployment.

Illustrative example

Multi-room dental practice — Wi-Fi modernization

The situation: a 12-operatory dental office running on ISP-provided routers and consumer mesh. Imaging uploads stalled during peak hours; the front desk rebooted the network weekly.

The engagement: full UniFi Foundation — gateway, PoE switching, Wi-Fi 6 access points per zone, VLANs separating staff, imaging, guest, and voice traffic. Migrated overnight between patient days.

The outcome: seamless roaming across every operatory, imaging uploads that finish before the patient sits up, and zero network downtime during the migration itself.

This is a representative example of a standard Stage 1 engagement — not a specific client. Real case studies publish here as founding-client work completes.
